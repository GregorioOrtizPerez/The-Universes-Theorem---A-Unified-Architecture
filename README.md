# 🏛️ The Universes Theorem & Fx Engine

> **The Universes Theorem (Gregorio Ortiz Perez):** A deterministic, closed, tripartite architectural system ($U_1, U_2, U_3$) establishing structural collision elimination on the $U_1$ grid ($6k \pm 1$) with absolute precision (0.00% error rate).

---

## 📌 Abstract & Theoretical Framework

The Universes Theorem replaces statistical and probabilistic approximations with a exact structural geometry:
* **Structural Grid ($U_1$):** Elimination of composite candidates on the $6k \pm 1$ structure.
* **Tripartite Triangulation:** Mechanical collision resolution between families ($F_1, F_2, F_3$).
* **Deterministic Precision:** 0.00% structural error rate validated by the $Fx$ engine implementation.
